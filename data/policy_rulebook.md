# Synthetic reimbursement rulebook — Bramblewick Finance (fictional)

All rules below are invented for this portfolio project. They describe no
real employer policy.

## POL-MEAL-01
Meals are reimbursed up to 75 dollars per claim day, and a receipt is needed
for any single meal line above 25 dollars.

## POL-LODGE-01
Lodging is reimbursed up to 180 dollars per night, with a receipt required
for every night claimed.

## POL-OFFICE-01
Office supplies are reimbursed up to 500 dollars per claim, provided the
items are for business use and the invoice total reconciles.

## POL-TRAVEL-01
Travel is reimbursed up to 1000 dollars per claim, and a trip log is
required for mileage and transport lines.

## POL-RECEIPT-01
Any single line above 75 dollars must carry a receipt reference, or the
claim goes to human review.

## POL-THRESHOLD-01
Claims above 1000 dollars need a named-person approval before payment, and
that approval is single-use, scoped to the claim, and expiring.

## POL-DUP-01
A vendor invoice number may be reimbursed only once; a repeat of the same
vendor, invoice number, and total is rejected as a duplicate.
